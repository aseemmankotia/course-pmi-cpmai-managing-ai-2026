# Chapter 4 Lab: Business Case, ROI/TCO, Architecture, and Resourcing

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Build a simple TCO worksheet comparing three-year AI solution costs (compute, data labeling, staffing, maintenance) against projected business benefit for a sample use case, and produce a one-page high-level architecture sketch.

## Key takeaway

A credible AI business case requires full lifecycle TCO, honest KPI definition, and a resourced adoption/change plan — not just a compelling ROI number.
