# Chapter 8 Lab: Model Technique Selection and QA/QC

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given three use-case scenarios, select the most appropriate learning approach (supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement) and justify the interpretability trade-off in a short recommendation note.

## Key takeaway

Model technique selection is a joint PM/data-science decision balancing performance, complexity, and interpretability — and it must be governed by disciplined QA/QC from the start.
