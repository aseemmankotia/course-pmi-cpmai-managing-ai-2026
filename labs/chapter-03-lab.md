# Chapter 3 Lab: AI Feasibility, Risk Assessment, and Scope Definition

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a feasibility scorecard (technical, data, organizational, cost) for a sample customer-churn prediction proposal and draft a one-page scope statement with explicit in/out-of-scope items.

## Key takeaway

Feasibility and scope work exists to prevent AI projects from proceeding on optimism alone — every claim needs a documented, honest evaluation.
