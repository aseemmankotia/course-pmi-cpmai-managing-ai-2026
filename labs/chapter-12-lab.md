# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Test-Day Strategy

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a timed 20-question mixed-domain mini-simulation (weighted per official domain percentages) under strict 80-seconds-per-question pacing, then review a scoring rubric mapping each question back to its domain.

Exam-day success comes from disciplined pacing, phase/gate recognition, and understanding logistics at Pearson VUE or via OnVue — treat this simulation as reinforcement of PMI's own required prep course, not a replacement for it.
