# Chapter 11 Lab: Production Governance, Monitoring, and Closing the Loop

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Design a production monitoring dashboard outline (KPIs plus model performance metrics and alert thresholds) and draft a transition-plan summary handing the model from project team to operations, including defined support roles.

## Key takeaway

Operationalization does not end at go-live — ongoing governance, drift monitoring, and a tested contingency plan are what sustain AI value and manage risk in production.
