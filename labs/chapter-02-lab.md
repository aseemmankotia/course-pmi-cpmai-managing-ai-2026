# Chapter 2 Lab: Framing the Business Problem and the Seven Patterns of AI

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given five short business-problem statements, classify each into one of the Seven Patterns of AI and justify the mapping in a one-paragraph rationale document.

## Key takeaway

Every AI project starts by correctly framing the business problem and matching it to one of the Seven Patterns — get this wrong and every later phase inherits the error.
