# Chapter 1 Lab: Responsible AI Foundations: Privacy, Bias, and Governance

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Build a one-page AI Transparency & Bias Checklist for a hypothetical hiring-screening model, mapping each governance control (PIA, encryption, audit trail, bias metric) to a CPMAI lifecycle phase.

## Key takeaway

Responsible AI is not a final checklist — it is continuous governance across privacy, transparency, bias, and accountability woven through every CPMAI phase.
