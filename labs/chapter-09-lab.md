# Chapter 9 Lab: Training Management, Data Preparation, and the Two Go/No-Go Gates

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a model-readiness go/no-go checklist for a sample model, evaluating performance against success criteria, robustness/generalization evidence, and documentation completeness before recommending proceed/hold.

## Key takeaway

CPMAI enforces two distinct go/no-go gates in Domain IV — data readiness for preparation and model readiness for operationalization — and both require documented, evidence-based approval.
