## 🏆 Certification Prep

These materials are designed to help you prepare for the **PMI Certified Professional in Managing AI (PMI-CPMAI) exam preparation - PMI's certification for managing AI initiatives end to end using the CPMAI Methodology (originally from Cognilytica, acquired by PMI in 2024): responsible AI, business and data understanding, data preparation, model development and evaluation, and operationalization, with the go/no-go gate discipline that distinguishes AI projects from traditional software projects** exam. Exam mechanics change — always confirm the current outline, format, and fees on the official certification page before registering.
