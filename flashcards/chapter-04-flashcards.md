## Chapter 4: Business Case, ROI/TCO, Architecture, and Resourcing — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | ROI formula for an AI business case? | ROI = (Net Benefit − Cost) / Cost. Exam tip: benefit must be tied to a measurable business outcome, not just model accuracy. |
| 2 | List the 4 core TCO components for an AI project. | Compute/infrastructure, labor (data scientists/engineers/MLOps), data acquisition/labeling, and ongoing maintenance/retraining. Exam tip: maintenance is the most commonly omitted category. |
| 3 | Why is AI TCO different from traditional software TCO? | AI requires recurring costs for model monitoring, drift detection, and periodic retraining — traditional software TCO ends largely after deployment/patching. |
| 4 | ADKAR change management model — what do the 5 letters stand for? | Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement. Exam tip: fear-of-job-loss resistance maps to the 'Desire' stage, not 'Awareness'. |
| 5 | A user resists an AI tool but says 'I understand what it does, I just don't want it.' Which ADKAR stage is failing? | Desire. Awareness (understanding) is already present; the gap is motivational. |
| 6 | What must a high-level AI architecture diagram include beyond data ingestion, training, and serving? | A monitoring/feedback loop for drift detection and retraining triggers. Without it, the architecture is not production-ready. |
| 7 | Data Engineer vs. Data Scientist — what's the key resourcing distinction tested on the exam? | Data Engineer = builds/maintains data pipelines and infrastructure. Data Scientist = builds/evaluates models. A pipeline gap is a data engineering gap, not a data science gap. |
| 8 | Why is 'model accuracy = 90%' alone a poor KPI for an AI business case? | It's a technical metric not tied to a business outcome (cost, revenue, time saved). Exam tip: KPIs must link model performance to measurable business impact. |
| 9 | Build vs. Buy TCO comparison — what's the exam trap? | Comparing only upfront licensing cost vs. initial dev cost. Correct approach: compare compute, labor, data prep, integration, and maintenance over the same time horizon for both options. |
| 10 | Which labor roles belong in a complete AI project labor cost estimate? | Data scientists, data engineers, ML/DevOps engineers, and business analysts supporting change management/adoption — not just the modeling team. |
| 11 | What 3 resource gap categories does CPMAI ask you to assess during resourcing planning? | Team/skills gap, hardware/infrastructure gap, and contractor/vendor gap. |
| 12 | What is the most commonly missed cost driver causing AI projects to be flagged 'unprofitable' after launch? | Ongoing maintenance and retraining labor — an operational cost missing from the original ROI/TCO business case. |
