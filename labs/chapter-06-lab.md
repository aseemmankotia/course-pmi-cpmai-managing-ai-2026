# Chapter 6 Lab: AI Workspace, Infrastructure, and Data Gathering

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Draft an AI workspace provisioning checklist covering compute, storage, secure access, and version-control tooling, then design a data refresh schedule for a use case requiring near-real-time feeds.

## Key takeaway

A properly provisioned, version-controlled AI workspace with validated, refreshable data feeds prevents costly rework later in the lifecycle.
