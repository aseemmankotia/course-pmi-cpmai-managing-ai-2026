## Chapter 7: Data Evaluation, Compliance, and the Data Go/No-Go Decision — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | What are the four core data quality dimensions heavily tested on the CPMAI exam? | Accuracy, Completeness, Consistency, Timeliness. Exam tip: memorize as 'ACCT' — each maps to a distinct real-world problem type (correctness, missingness, uniformity, currency). |
| 2 | Accuracy vs. Consistency — what's the exam distinction? | Accuracy = does the value match real-world truth? Consistency = is the same entity/value represented the same way across records/sources? Exam trap: renamed codes for the same entity = consistency, not accuracy. |
| 3 | What is the 'data go/no-go decision gate' in CPMAI? | A formal checkpoint at the end of Data Understanding where the team decides to proceed to Data Preparation, redefine scope, seek alternate data, or halt the project based on data quality, bias, and compliance findings. |
| 4 | Before using any data source in an AI project, what three checks must be completed (privacy/licensing/compliance)? | 1) Consent/legal basis for data use, 2) Licensing terms of the data source (ToS, copyright, usage rights), 3) Regulatory compliance (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA as applicable). Exam tip: public availability ≠ legal permission to use. |
| 5 | What is the purpose of bias/gap analysis during EDA? | To compare distributions and outcomes across subgroups (demographics, categories) to detect underrepresentation or disparate impact before model training, preventing biased AI outcomes. |
| 6 | Name three common EDA techniques tested on CPMAI. | 1) Summary statistics (mean, median, std dev), 2) Visualizations (histograms, box plots, scatter plots), 3) Missing-value/heatmap profiling. Used to assess distribution, outliers, and gaps before modeling. |
| 7 | Distribution analysis reveals unnatural clustering of values (e.g., many exact round numbers). What quality issue does this suggest? | An accuracy problem — values were likely estimated, defaulted, or rounded rather than measured, meaning they don't reflect real-world truth. |
| 8 | What should you do if EDA reveals significant data gaps and undocumented consent issues, but leadership wants to proceed quickly? | Recommend 'No-Go' or a scope redefinition — halting or remediating is required until compliance/quality risks are resolved or alternate data is sourced. Don't proceed just to meet a deadline. |
| 9 | How should data quality findings be communicated to non-technical executives per CPMAI best practice? | Translate technical findings into business impact and risk language (e.g., '40% missing data limits accurate prediction for a third of customers') rather than presenting raw statistics or code. |
| 10 | Completeness vs. Timeliness — what's the exam distinction? | Completeness = are values present (no missing data)? Timeliness = is the data current/up-to-date and available when needed? Exam trap: old-but-complete data still fails on timeliness. |
| 11 | What is the first step of EDA before any modeling decision? | Data profiling — generating summary statistics and visualizations to understand distributions, outliers, and missingness. Modeling should never precede this step in the CPMAI flow. |
| 12 | Why is 'public data' not automatically safe to use for AI training? | Public visibility doesn't grant legal usage rights — platform Terms of Service, copyright, and privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA) can still restrict AI training use even on openly viewable data. |
