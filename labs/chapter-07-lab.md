# Chapter 7 Lab: Data Evaluation, Compliance, and the Data Go/No-Go Decision

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Perform a mock EDA summary on a sample dataset description, flag at least two quality/bias gaps, and write the executive-facing go/no-go recommendation memo.

## Key takeaway

The data go/no-go gate exists to stop AI projects from building on data that is incomplete, biased, or non-compliant — sponsor time pressure never overrides this checkpoint.
